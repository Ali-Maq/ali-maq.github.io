## Graduate research mentorship

**Carnegie Mellon capstone at Mount Sinai · September–December 2024**

I mentored six master's students on CAR-T toxicity prediction using wearable signals and cytokine data. I guided experiment design, baseline comparisons, and reporting, and coordinated project scope with the clinical team.

During my time at Mount Sinai, I also helped research colleagues use and evaluate AI tools, with documentation and guidance on retrieval, model outputs, and source checking.

## Teaching at NYU

**Teaching Assistant · Data Structures & Algorithms for Bioinformatics · Spring 2022–Spring 2023**

I supported Professor Manpreet S. Katari's graduate course from Spring 2022 to Spring 2023, working with more than 130 master's students. I taught algorithms and data structures, developed assignments connecting computer science with bioinformatics, and supported students through office hours and project feedback.

## Topics I enjoy teaching

- Designing agent workflows and evaluating their outputs.
- Retrieval, source attribution, and scientific evidence extraction.
- Model adaptation and comparisons against meaningful baselines.
- Algorithms and reproducible research software.

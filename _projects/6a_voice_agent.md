## What I built

I fine-tuned **Whisper Small** for recognition of multiple myeloma terminology, with on-device use as the target. The prototype focused on terms such as drug names and biomarkers that general speech-recognition systems can misrecognize.

This project explored domain adaptation and the practical constraints of local inference. No public benchmark or deployment-scale result is claimed here.

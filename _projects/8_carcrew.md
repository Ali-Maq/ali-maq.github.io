## What I built

As **Technical Lead on Carcrew’s founding team (August 2016–September 2017)**, I built the MVP of an automotive repair marketplace using Django, Flask, and PostgreSQL.

I led **three developers** on a backend holding **more than 10 million parts-inventory records**. The work combined product development, backend implementation, and coordination of a small engineering team.

## Scale and delivery

The platform grew to **10,000+ daily active users**. I also supported technical due diligence for a **$2 million Series A**.

This was an early end-to-end engineering role: building the initial product, coordinating implementation, and supporting its growth. It established experience in software delivery and technical leadership that I later brought to research engineering.

## Research focus

I contributed to collaborative research that combines tumor and immune data to study heterogeneity in multiple myeloma. This work connects computational analysis with questions about disease subtypes and treatment response.

## Publications

- **IMS 2025 conference abstract:** integrating the tumor microenvironment with tumor multi-omic data using unsupervised machine learning.
- **2026 conference abstract:** multi-modal single-cell profiling of MRD-negative patients to study immune signatures associated with prolonged response or relapse.

These abstracts describe research findings, rather than validated treatment-selection tools.

## Research focus

PRIME studies predictive relapse indicators in patients with multiple myeloma receiving BCMA- or GPRC5D-targeting T-cell engagers. I am a contributing author on this collaborative modeling work.

The study uses clinical and laboratory predictors in a survival-modeling framework. The published abstract reports internal validation; further validation is needed before making broad claims about generalization.

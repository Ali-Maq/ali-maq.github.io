CAR-T Wearables & ML

Data analysis for a published pilot study of cytokine release syndrome

My contribution

I contributed data analysis and machine-learning work to a prospective, single-center pilot study of wearables and cytokine profiling after CAR-T therapy for myeloma. I also mentored six Carnegie Mellon graduate students on related modeling work in September–December 2024.

Study findings

The study enrolled 30 patients, with 25 evaluable. Wearable monitoring detected 18 of 20 CRS episodes, with a median lead of about 7 hours before nursing detection.

Wearable detection and predictive models are separate analyses. Combined wearable/cytokine models reported 85% and 81% accuracy for the two CAR-T products, with sensitivities of 50% and 38%. That tradeoff matters: accuracy alone does not describe how reliably an early-warning model detects events.

These findings come from a small pilot cohort and require broader validation.
